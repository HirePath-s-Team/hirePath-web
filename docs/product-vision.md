## Prep Analytics Suite Product Vision

### Summary
Prep Analytics Suite is a logged-in, multi-domain interview preparation platform. Users pick a domain (SWE, Data Analyst, etc.) and target companies, then receive a company-aware, step-by-step roadmap with practice questions, learning modules, and analytics-driven coaching.

### North Star
Help users know exactly what to study next and measure readiness for target companies in their chosen domain.

### Core User Flow
1. Sign up / log in
2. Choose domain, target companies, and timeline
3. Get a personalized roadmap (weekly plan + milestones)
4. Learn concepts and practice questions tied to the roadmap
5. Track analytics (strengths, gaps, streaks, confidence)
6. Iterate with adaptive recommendations

### Primary Sections (matching current UI)
- Dashboard: daily plan, streaks, progress, coaching cues
- Learn: concept modules and patterns
- Questions: practice sets with hints/solutions
- Roadmaps: stepwise weekly plan per domain + company
- Companies: company-specific topic breakdowns and recent questions
- Analytics: performance, mastery gaps, and next actions
- Bookmarks: saved questions and notes
- Profile/Settings: goals, preferences, and account

### Data Pillars
- Domain-focused roadmaps
- Company-aware topic frequency
- Structured question content + solutions
- Analytics + coaching signals

### Success Metrics
- Weekly active users and streak length
- Roadmap completion rate
- Accuracy improvements by topic
- Session completion and time-on-task
